A ten-year journey from simple mechanisms to autonomous systems. One tool at a time, shipping a physical project every year.

GRADES 3–5 Ages 8–11

Foundations

Paper mechanisms, DC motors, copper tape circuits, and block logic on the BBC micro:bit.

TOOLS: micro:bit · Motors · Sonar
GRADES 6–7 Ages 11–13

The Bridge

Text-based Python syntax, I2C digital buses, ESP32 microcontrollers, and cloud telemetry.

Engineering

TOOLS: C++ · PID Math · Edge ML

Frontier

TOOLS: ROS 2 · LiDAR · Linux
