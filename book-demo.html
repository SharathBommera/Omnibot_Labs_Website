THE 45-MINUTE PROMISE

Build something real in the first 45 minutes.

We never subject students or parents to passive slide presentations. During the free demo class, your student will have physical components in hand, building a real functioning motor circuit or sensor system before leaving the workshop.

  • Zero Prior Experience Needed: We calibrate to the student's exact grade and comfort level.
  • Component-Level Hardware: Real breadboards, wires, and microcontrollers.
  • Individual Mentorship: 1-on-1 or micro-cohort of at most 3 students per builder.
  • No Hidden Obligations: Completely free orientation session.
