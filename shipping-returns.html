Shipping & Replacement Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · OmniBot Labs, Hyderabad, India

1. Pan-India Dispatch

All physical hardware kits, sensor packs, and institutional bundles are assembled, inspected, and dispatched directly from our central robotics lab in Hyderabad, Telangana. We partner with tier-1 insured express couriers (BlueDart, DTDC, Delhivery, Speed Post).

2. Delivery Timelines

• Metro Cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai): 2 to 4 business days.
• Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 4 to 6 business days.
• Bulk Institutional Shipments (20+ kits): Scheduled dispatch based on academic start dates.

3. Request-Based Order Coordination

Because hardware stock fluctuates based on workshop cohort allocations, our website uses a direct WhatsApp request flow. When you send your cart request, our team confirms exact stock numbers and shares an official GST invoice before shipping.

4. Dead-On-Arrival (DOA) Hardware Replacement

Electronic parts can occasionally sustain transit damage. If any microcontroller, servo motor, sensor breakout, or display module arrives non-functional, notify us via WhatsApp or email within 7 days of package delivery. We dispatch replacement components immediately at zero additional courier cost.

5. Support & Tracking Inquiries

For shipping status updates or tracking AWB numbers, reach our dispatch desk on WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210 or email hello@omnibotlabs.com.
