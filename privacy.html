PRIVACY & DATA RESPECT

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · OmniBot Labs, Hyderabad, India

1. Our Core Principle: Zero Friction, Zero Exploitation

The public OmniBot Labs platform is entirely login-less. We do not require accounts, passwords, or personal profiles to browse our curriculum, view student projects, inspect hardware specifications, or add kits to an enquiry cart.

2. Client-Side Persistence (LocalStorage)

We store your dark/light theme preference and your selected hardware items in your local browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to generate a WhatsApp request message.

3. Information Collected via Forms

When you voluntarily submit a Demo Class Request or an Institutional Partnership Inquiry, we collect only the necessary contact details (such as parent name, email, phone number, and student grade) required to schedule and coordinate the session. We never sell, lease, or monetize this information with third-party advertisers.

4. WhatsApp Communication

When you use our WhatsApp request feature, our website opens WhatsApp using standard URL schemes. You maintain complete control over the message sent and any subsequent conversation with our business desk.

5. Questions & Contact

For any privacy-related queries, reach out directly to hello@omnibotlabs.com.
