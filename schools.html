INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERSHIP PATHWAY

Build a World-Class Robotics & AI Lab on Your Campus

We are an implementation partner, not just a kit vendor. OmniBot Labs designs physical maker spaces, provisions classroom hardware batches, trains campus faculty, integrates the 10-year Builder’s Ladder, and executes high-energy annual student exhibitions.

01 // LAB ARCHITECTURE

Physical Space & Provisioning

We design ergonomic workbenches, component organizer caddies, safety gear stations, and provide durable, solderless robot hardware sets for cohorts of 20 to 100 students.

02 // PEDAGOGY

10-Year Curriculum Integration

Full NEP 2020 alignment. Structured grade-by-grade syllabi with student logbooks, slide decks, and grading rubrics that evaluate physical builds rather than rote tests.

03 // TRAINERS

Faculty Enablement or Master Trainers

Choose between having certified OmniBot Master Trainers conduct weekly lab sessions on campus, or enrolling your existing science and IT faculty in our rigorous summer bootcamps.

TURNKEY DEPLOYMENT PROCESS

How We Deploy on Your Campus

WEEK 1–2 // DISCOVERY

1. Space Assessment

Review campus layout, electrical drops, student cohort sizes, and academic timetable slotting.

WEEK 3–4 // PROVISIONING

2. Hardware Dispatch

Delivery of organized chassis kits, sensor fusion cases, student logbooks, and redundant spare components.

WEEK 5 // ENABLEMENT

3. Teacher Bootcamps

Hands-on faculty intensive: teachers build the exact robot capstones and practice lab safety protocols.

ONGOING // MENTORSHIP

4. Weekly Guided Labs

Students climb one rung of the ladder each week, recording measurements and building physical prototypes.

FINALE // CELEBRATION

5. Annual Demo Day

On-campus public robotics league and capstone defense where families and invited industry judges evaluate builds.
