Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026 · OmniBot Labs, Hyderabad, India

1. Purpose of the Platform

OmniBot Labs provides educational curriculum information, hardware specifications, and an enquiry-based commerce interface for educational kits and maker tools. No automatic payments or digital contracts are executed through this web portal.

2. Hardware Kit Use & Safety

All OmniBot hardware kits are intended for educational experimentation. Beginner and intermediate kits operate on low DC voltages (3.3V–12V). Adult supervision is recommended for elementary school students (Grades 3–5). Soldering irons and high-heat tools are restricted to advanced engineering courses under trained instructor supervision.

3. Intellectual Property & Open Source

Our 10-year Builder’s Ladder curriculum structure is proprietary to OmniBot Labs. However, all hardware schematics, laser chassis files, and sample software sketches published under our Open Builder Repository are released under Creative Commons CC-BY-SA and MIT open-source licenses for educational empowerment.

4. Institutional Agreements

School and college lab implementations are governed by separate, formal Memorandums of Understanding (MoU) defining hardware schedules, trainer deployments, and academic timetables.
